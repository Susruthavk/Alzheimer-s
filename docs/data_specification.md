# ALZ-301 Simulated Data Specification

Version 0.1, data cutoff 2026-10-31, default seed 301. Generated by `python/simulate_data.py`.
Everything marked **Assumption** is a modelling choice, not a real protocol requirement.

## Study design

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Design | 52-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel group |
| Arms | PBO Placebo, LOW ALZ-301 Low Dose, HIGH ALZ-301 High Dose |
| Randomization | 1:1:1, permuted blocks of 6, one list per site |
| Target enrollment | 240 randomized (site targets sum to 240) |
| Study start | 2025-02-03; sites activate in a staggered way over 4 months |
| Population | Early AD, screening MMSE 20 to 26 (inclusion) |

## Subject flow (Assumptions)

- Screening arrivals per site follow a Poisson process from site activation to cutoff, weekdays only.
- Consent is 0 to 3 days before the screening visit. Baseline is 7 to 28 days after screening.
- Screen failure 38% (reasons: amyloid PET negative 45%, MMSE outside 20-26 25%, other criteria 20%, consent withdrawn 10%).
- Randomization happens on the baseline visit date.
- Before each post-baseline visit there is a 3% chance of discontinuation; no visits after it.
- 2% of post-baseline visits are missed (recorded with status Missed, no date).
- Visits that would fall after the cutoff are not generated. Subjects with baseline after the cutoff stay in status Screening.

## eCRF entry and SDV (Assumptions)

- Entry lag per form is geometric with the site's mean. Forms whose entry date would be after the cutoff do not exist yet (these are recent, not "missing").
- 85% of entered forms are source-data verified 20 to 90 days after entry, if that is before the cutoff.
- Only cognitive assessment totals carry values. DM, ELIG, VS and AE are tracked at form level only.
- Manual CRA queries: 4% of forms get one, opened 1 to 60 days after entry; 80% answered within 21 days; 85% of those closed within 14 days.

## Cognitive scores (Assumptions)

Each subject has a true baseline and a true annual slope; observed score = true + slope x years + noise.

| Instrument | Range | Baseline | Placebo slope per year | Noise SD | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MMSE | 0-30, integer | ~N(23.5, 2.0) | -2.4 | 1.2 | lower = worse |
| CDR-SB | 0-18, steps of 0.5 | 3.0 + 0.35 x (24 - MMSE0) + N(0, 1), clipped 0.5-9 | +1.5 | 0.5 | higher = worse |
| ADAS-Cog 13 | 0-85, integer | 27 + 1.6 x (24 - MMSE0) + N(0, 5), clipped 10-60 | +5.5 | 2.0 | higher = worse |

- Slopes vary between subjects (lognormal, SD 0.4 on the log scale).
- APOE4 carriers (60%) decline 20% faster.
- Low dose slows decline by 15%, high dose by 30%.

## Planted issues (ground truth in `sim.injected_issues`)

Rates are per opportunity, times the site's quality factor. Planted issues never overlap on the same visit.

| issue_type | subtype | Rate | How it is planted |
|---|---|---|---|
| OUT_OF_WINDOW | EARLY / LATE | 3.5% of completed post-baseline visits | visit date moved 3 to 21 days outside its window (related form and assessment dates move with it) |
| MISSING_ECRF | | 1.5% of entered forms on visits more than 30 days before cutoff | form row (and its assessment and queries) deleted |
| IMPLAUSIBLE_CHANGE | MMSE / CDRSB / ADAS13 | 1.2% of assessments with a prior one | score set to previous ± 7-10 (MMSE), 4-6 (CDR-SB), 15-22 (ADAS-Cog 13) |
| INCONSISTENT_DATE | VISIT_BEFORE_CONSENT | 1.5% of subjects | consent date set 2 to 10 days after screening |
| INCONSISTENT_DATE | RANDOMIZATION_MISMATCH | 1.5% of randomized subjects | randomization date shifted 3 to 15 days from the baseline visit |
| INCONSISTENT_DATE | VISIT_AFTER_DISCONTINUATION | 25% of discontinued subjects | discontinuation date set before the last visit |
| INCONSISTENT_DATE | ASSESSMENT_DATE_MISMATCH | 0.8% of post-screening assessments | assessment dated 5 to 40 days before its visit |
| INCONSISTENT_DATE | ENTRY_BEFORE_VISIT | 0.2% of entered forms | entry date 1 to 10 days before the visit |

Clean simulated data has no out-of-window visits and no missing eCRFs older than 30 days, so those
checks should find exactly the planted ones. Score-change checks (thresholds MMSE ≥5, CDR-SB ≥3,
ADAS-Cog 13 ≥12 between consecutive assessments) can also flag a few natural large changes; tests
will check that every planted one is found, not that every flag is planted. A planted score jump
usually also flags the following pair, as a real transcription error would.
